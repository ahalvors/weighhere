Public scales near West Monroe, Monroe and Tallulah, Louisiana

Five CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages along I-20 in northeast Louisiana: Pilot #428 in West Monroe (Exit 112), Love’s #806 in east Monroe (Exit 124), Pilot #335 in Rayville (Exit 138), and a pair at Tallulah Exit 171 — Love’s #237 and TA #0046 — the last CAT stops before the Mississippi River bridge to Vicksburg. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves around Monroe and the University of Louisiana Monroe, RVs and boat trailers on I-20 between Shreveport and Jackson, and horse or livestock trailers headed to the Delta.

This page lists 5 places to weigh near Monroe, West Monroe & Tallulah, LA: 5 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in West Monroe, Monroe, Rayville and Tallulah. 5 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

Do not go here for a ticket

Louisiana commercial vehicle enforcement weigh stations and inspection pull-offs on I-20 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weight ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
