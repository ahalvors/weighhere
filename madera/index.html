CAT Scale at Love’s and Pilot

We list only corridor CAT stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Love’s #736 (Madera / CA-99 Exit 157) and Pilot #365 (Madera / CA-99 Exit 159) each list CAT Scale / CAT Scales. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. In California, go inside for a printed weighmaster certificate when you need one. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

California Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Facilities (CHP weigh stations) are for commercial enforcement — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, horse trailer, or PPM load. See Caltrans’ weigh-station primer and follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Source: Caltrans — Weigh-Stations (Enforcement Facilities)
