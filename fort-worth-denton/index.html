Public scales on I-35W from south Fort Worth to Denton, Texas

Four CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages along the I-35W corridor: Love’s #281 at Exit 40 on the south side of Fort Worth, Pilot #434 at Exit 65 by Alliance, and TA #0266 and Love’s #609 facing each other at Exit 471 in Denton. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves around the Metroplex (including Naval Air Station Fort Worth JRB households), RVs and fifth-wheels heading north toward Oklahoma, and horse and stock trailers.

This page lists 4 places to weigh near Fort Worth & Denton, TX: 4 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Fort Worth and Denton. 4 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites and roadside inspection pull-offs on I-35W, I-35, I-20 and I-820 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
