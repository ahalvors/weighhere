About WeighHere

WeighHere is your comprehensive resource for finding public vehicle scales across California and beyond. Whether you're traveling in an RV, driving a commercial truck, or need to weigh a trailer, we help you locate certified public scales near you.

Why Weigh Your Vehicle?

Knowing your vehicle's weight is crucial for several reasons:

  • Safety: Operating an overloaded vehicle is dangerous and can lead to accidents, brake failure, and tire blowouts
  • Legal Compliance: Commercial vehicles must comply with federal and state weight regulations
  • Vehicle Performance: Proper weight distribution improves handling, braking, and fuel efficiency
  • Manufacturer Limits: Exceeding your vehicle's Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR) can void warranties and cause damage
  • RV Safety: RV owners need to ensure they're not overloading axles or exceeding tire weight ratings

Our Data Sources

WeighHere aggregates scale information from multiple trusted sources:

  • CDFA (California Department of Food and Agriculture): Certified commercial scales
  • CAT Scale Network: Nationwide certified scale locations at truck stops
  • Public Weigh Stations: State-operated and commercial facilities open to the public
  • Community Contributions: Verified reports from RV and truck communities

🔍 Data Accuracy

We strive to maintain accurate and current information, but scale locations, hours, and pricing can change. Always call ahead to verify availability and confirm that a scale can accommodate your vehicle type and size.

Types of Scales

Certified Scales: CDFA or state-certified scales that provide official weight certificates, often required for commercial purposes.

CAT Scales: Part of a nationwide network of certified scales, typically located at truck stops. Known for consistent pricing and guaranteed weight tickets.

Public Scales: Facilities open to the general public, including RV owners and non-commercial vehicles.

Axle Scales: Some locations offer individual axle weighing, which is particularly important for RVs to ensure proper weight distribution.

Pricing

Scale prices vary by location and type:

  • CAT Scales: Typically $14 for the first weigh, $2.50 for reweigh within 24 hours
  • Certified Scales: $10-25 depending on location and services
  • Public Weigh Stations: Some are free, others charge nominal fees

Avoiding Enforcement Scales

⚠️ Important Notice

Some weigh stations are operated by California Highway Patrol (CHP) or other law enforcement agencies and are not open to the public for voluntary weighing. These locations are clearly marked in our database to help you avoid confusion.
