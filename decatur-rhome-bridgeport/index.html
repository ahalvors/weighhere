Public scales on US-287 from Rhome to Decatur, Texas, plus Bridgeport on US-380

Four CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages in Wise County, the first stretch of US-287 north of Fort Worth: Love’s #380 and Pilot #1140 in Rhome, TA Express #0456 in Decatur, and Love’s #713 in Bridgeport on US-380. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves out of north Fort Worth, RVs and fifth-wheels heading to Lake Bridgeport or up US-287 to the Panhandle, and horse and stock trailers.

This page lists 4 places to weigh near Decatur, Rhome & Bridgeport, TX: 4 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Rhome, Decatur and Bridgeport. 4 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites and roadside inspection pull-offs on US-287, US-81 and US-380 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
