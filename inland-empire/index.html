Public scales in the Inland Empire

Colton has a ScaleRegistry public house. CDFA’s Riverside (c=33) and San Bernardino (c=36) grids both loaded this compile — Blythe Public Scales is the dedicated walk-up house on Riverside (full write-up on Coachella Valley / I-10). New west-IE CAT cluster on operator pages: TA Ontario, Petro Ontario, Flying J #1009 Mira Loma, Pilot #1326 Colton (corridor write-up on Ontario / I-10 West). Also Love’s Coachella, Flying J Thousand Palms, Pilot North Palm Springs, Pilot Mecca, Pilot Rialto, Pilot Perris, Flying J Fontana, and the I-15 High Desert CAT cluster. Riverside County landfills have scales for dump traffic — call first, they are not ticket shops. Corridor pages: Ontario / I-10 West · Coachella Valley / I-10 · I-15 / High Desert.

ScaleRegistry lists Superior Scale House in Colton the same way it lists the Lancaster 80' house we already carry. It does not publish hours, fees, or a phone. CDFA Riverside also names Blythe Public Scales, LLC — that dedicated house is featured on the Coachella Valley / I-10 page (and appears in this county filter). We did not invent hours or fees. Call first — Riverside County landfills

That is not a promise they will issue a weighmaster certificate to a walk-up U-Haul, RV, or horse trailer.
