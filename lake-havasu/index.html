Lake Havasu City · I-40 Exit 9 · AZ-95 · Mohave County · listings checked 8 Oct 2026

Public scales at Lake Havasu / I-40 Exit 9

Two CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages at the I-40 / AZ-95 junction (Exit 9) north of Lake Havasu City: Pilot Travel Center #211 and Love’s Travel Stop #386. Handy for boat trailers, RVs, and toy haulers heading to the lake, and for U-Haul or PPM moves crossing the Colorado River on I-40. No ScaleRegistry dedicated walk-up house here and no Arizona CDFA-equivalent facility grid. For Kingman / I-40 east, use Kingman / I-40; for Needles and the California side, use I-15 / High Desert or Mojave / Hwy 58.

CAT Scales at Pilot and Love’s

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Pilot #211 (14750 AZ-95) lists CAT Scale and answers yes to “has a CAT scale”; Love’s #386 (14875 AZ Hwy-95) lists CAT Scales among Select Amenities. Both sit on AZ-95 at I-40 Exit 9. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Boat trailers: weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. Arizona weighmaster rules differ from California — confirm ticket needs with your TO or DMV equivalent. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

Arizona ports of entry and highway weigh / weigh-in-motion sites on I-40 are commercial enforcement — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
