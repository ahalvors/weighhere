Public scales on I-20 in the Permian Basin: Monahans, Odessa, Midland and Big Spring

Eleven CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages along about 100 miles of I-20: Pilot #1205 at Monahans (Exit 86); Pilot #1161 (Exit 108), Love’s #339 (Exit 115) and Flying J #580 (Exit 121) at Odessa; Pilot #257 (Exit 126), Love’s #623 (Exit 131), Flying J #1033 (Exit 137B) and Pilot #1145 (TX-191) at Midland; and Love’s #976 (Exit 176), TA (Exit 177) and Pilot #559 (Exit 178) at Big Spring. CAT Scales in Monahans, Odessa, Midland and Big Spring

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. All three Love’s pages and all seven Pilot Flying J pages list a CAT Scale and say the store is open 24 hours; TA Big Spring lists a CAT Scale and 24/7 fuel but no 24-hour store statement. None publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Pilot #1145 sits on TX-191 between the two cities rather than on I-20. Not every Pilot here has a scale: Pilot #1227 Pyote (Exit 66), Pilot #1207 Monahans (Business I-20 & TX-464), Pilot #1209 Midland (Exit 136, Rankin Hwy), and Pilot #1354 and #1143 in Odessa (US-385 and TX-302) list no CAT on their own pages, so they are not here. West on I-20 toward Pecos, see Van Horn / Fort Stockton / Pecos. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites and roadside inspection pull-offs on I-20 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
