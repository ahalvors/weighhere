Public scales in Medford and the Rogue Valley (Central Point & Phoenix, I-5)

Two CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages, nine miles apart on I-5 through the Rogue Valley: Pilot #391 Central Point (Exit 33, north side of Medford) and Petro Phoenix #0324 (Exit 24 at Fern Valley Rd, south side of Medford). Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves into southern Oregon, RVs and fifth-wheels before or after the Siskiyou Summit, and horse and stock trailers out of the valley. South over the pass, see Weed / Yreka / I-5; north, see Albany / Eugene / Roseburg; east on OR-140, see Madras / Klamath Falls / US-97.

CAT Scales at Central Point and Phoenix

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Those are store hours, not a published CAT-staffing schedule. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not go here for a ticket

Oregon DOT weigh stations and ports of entry on I-5 (including the Ashland port near the California line) are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
