Do not go here for a ticket

California Commercial Vehicle Enforcement Facilities (CHP weigh stations) — including Grapevine-area enforcement sites — are for commercial enforcement, not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, horse trailer, or PPM load. See Caltrans’ weigh-station primer and follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Source: Caltrans — Weigh-Stations (Enforcement Facilities)
