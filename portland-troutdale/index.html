Public scales near Portland, OR (Troutdale & Aurora)

Three CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages around the Portland metro: TA Troutdale #0183 and Love’s #449 Troutdale, both at I-84 Exit 17 on the east edge of town, and TA Aurora #0056 at I-5 Exit 278 south of Wilsonville. Troutdale and Aurora are about 29 miles apart as the crow flies. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves into Portland, Gresham, Beaverton, Vancouver WA or the north Willamette Valley, RVs heading up the Columbia River Gorge on I-84, and horse trailers on I-5. CAT Scales in Troutdale and Aurora

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. TA Troutdale and TA Aurora list CAT Scale on TA Petro’s own pages and show the location as Open 24 Hours; Love’s #449 lists CAT Scales in its amenities and lists the store as Open 24-hours. Those are store hours, not a published CAT-staffing schedule. If one Troutdale scale is busy, the other is at the same exit. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. Oregon weighmaster rules differ from California — confirm ticket needs with your TO or licensing office. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

Oregon DOT weigh stations on I-5 and I-84 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
