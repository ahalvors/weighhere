Public scales on I-35 from Hillsboro through Waco to Troy, Texas

Six CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages along I-35 in Central Texas: Petro #0533 at Carl’s Corner (I-35E Exit 374), TA #0333 (Exit 370) and Love’s #231 (Exit 368A) in Hillsboro, Pilot #739 (Exit 331) and Pilot #432 (Exit 328) on the south side of Waco, and Love’s #719 in Troy (Exit 308 northbound / 306 southbound) between Waco and Temple. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves between Dallas–Fort Worth and Austin (including Fort Cavazos households heading north), RVs and fifth-wheels, and horse and stock trailers.

This page lists 6 places to weigh near Hillsboro & Waco, TX: 6 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Hillsboro, Waco and Troy. 6 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites and roadside inspection pull-offs on I-35, I-35E and I-35W are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
