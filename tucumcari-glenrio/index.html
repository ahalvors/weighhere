Public scales on I-40 in eastern New Mexico (Tucumcari & Glenrio)

Two CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages on I-40 between Santa Rosa and the Texas line: Love’s #262 Tucumcari (Exit 333) and TA Glenrio #0471 (Exit 369, right at the state line). Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves to or from Amarillo and Cannon AFB country, RVs and fifth-wheels on old Route 66, and horse and stock trailers crossing the eastern plains. West, see Moriarty / Santa Rosa / I-40 and Albuquerque / I-40 · I-25.

This page lists 2 places to weigh near Tucumcari & Glenrio, NM: 2 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Tucumcari and Glenrio. 2 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales on I-40 in eastern New Mexico

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Love’s #262 lists CAT Scales among its amenities and says its store and truck care are open 24 hours. TA Glenrio lists CAT Scale among its amenities, but the only hours it prints are restaurant hours (some days just “Call For Hours”), so we leave its scale hours blank — it is a remote stop, so call ahead. Neither publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Love’s #285 Santa Rosa does not list CAT Scales, and we found no Pilot Flying J store page for Tucumcari, so neither is here. Tucumcari to Glenrio (Exit 333 to Exit 369) is about 36 miles; west of Tucumcari, the next verified CAT is TA Santa Rosa (Exit 277). 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. New Mexico weighmaster rules differ from California — confirm ticket needs with your TO or licensing office. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

New Mexico ports of entry on I-40 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
