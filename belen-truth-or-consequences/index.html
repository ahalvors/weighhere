Public scales on I-25 in central New Mexico (Belen & Truth or Consequences)

Two CAT Scales verified on Love’s own location pages on the roughly 220-mile stretch of I-25 between Albuquerque and Las Cruces: Love’s #726 Belen (Exit 191) and Love’s #1045 Truth or Consequences (Exit 79). Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves to or from Kirtland AFB, White Sands and Fort Bliss country, RVs and fifth-wheels headed for Elephant Butte, and horse and stock trailers on the Rio Grande corridor.

This page lists 2 places to weigh near Belen & Truth or Consequences, NM: 2 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Belen and Truth or Consequences. 2 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales on I-25 in central New Mexico

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Both Love’s pages list CAT Scales among their amenities and say the store is open 24 hours (Belen’s truck care runs 6 a.m. to 11 p.m.). Neither publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Truth or Consequences is the only verified CAT stop in the roughly 110 miles between Belen and Las Cruces, so plan your weigh there if you are heading south. North of Belen, Albuquerque stops are on Albuquerque / I-40 · I-25; south of Truth or Consequences, Las Cruces stops are on Las Cruces / Deming / Lordsburg / I-10. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
