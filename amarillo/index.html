Amarillo · I-40 · Potter County TX · listings checked 4 Nov 2026

Public scales around Amarillo (I-40 Exits 60–76)

Six CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages along I-40 through Amarillo: Love’s #250 at Exit 60 on the west side, then an east-side cluster of Love’s #200 and TA #0055 at Exit 74 (Whitaker Rd), Petro #0307 and Pilot #436 at Exit 75 (Lakeside Dr), and Flying J #723 at Exit 76. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves, RVs and fifth-wheels crossing the Panhandle, and horse and livestock trailers in ranch country.

This page lists 6 places to weigh near Amarillo, TX: 6 CAT or truck-stop scales. All are in Amarillo. 6 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales around Amarillo

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Both Love’s pages and both Pilot Flying J pages list a CAT Scale and say the store is open 24 hours; TA and Petro list a CAT Scale and 24/7 fuel but no 24-hour store statement. None publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. The east-side stops sit within about two miles of each other near the airport, so if one scale lane is busy, the next exit is close. Exit 60 is about 15 miles west. Love’s #261 on I-27 (Canyon Dr) and Love’s #229 at Exit 96 near Claude list no CAT Scale, so they are not here. West on I-40 into New Mexico, see Tucumcari / Glenrio / I-40. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites and roadside inspection pull-offs are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
