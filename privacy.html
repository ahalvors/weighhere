Legal

Privacy Policy

Plain terms about what WeighHere collects (not much) and how hosting works.

Overview

WeighHere is an independent directory of public scales. This site does not require accounts, logins, or personal data submission. We do not collect names or payment information. The only personal data we collect is an email address if you choose to join our mailing list (see below).

Email list

If you sign up for the free PPM weigh-day checklist, we store the email address you enter using our host’s form service (Netlify Forms). We use it only to send occasional scale and moving tips from WeighHere. We do not sell or share it. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe, or you can ask to be removed at any time through our contact page, and we will delete your address.

Analytics

WeighHere uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookieless and collects only aggregate page views and performance data. It does not track individual visitors across sites or sell visitor data. If you block JavaScript or use privacy extensions, the site works fine.

Hosting

WeighHere is hosted on Netlify and served via Cloudflare for the public domain weighhere.com. Both services process standard web server logs (IP addresses, user agents, requested URLs) as part of delivering pages. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. Netlify's privacy policy: netlify.com/privacy.

Cookies

WeighHere does not set cookies. Cloudflare and Netlify may set security or performance cookies as part of infrastructure. These are not tracking cookies.

Third-party links

This site links to Google Maps, operator websites, government databases (CDFA, Caltrans, ADOT), and CAT Scale. Those destinations have their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

Affiliate links

Scale pages include Amazon Associates search links in a marked “Towing gear” box. Clicking one passes WeighHere’s Associates tag to Amazon, and Amazon may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy. That is how affiliate programs work. As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. See Affiliate Disclosure for full details.

Data requests

WeighHere has no visitor accounts. The only personal data we hold is the email address of anyone who joins our mailing list; to have yours removed, email support@weighhere.com and we will delete it. If you have questions about how a listing was compiled or believe a listing incorrectly references you, contact support@weighhere.com.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the site evolves. The last-compiled date (10 Nov 2026) reflects the current site build. Material changes will be noted on the About page.
