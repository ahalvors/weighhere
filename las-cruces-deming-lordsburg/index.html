Public scales on I-10 in southern New Mexico (Las Cruces, Deming & Lordsburg)

Seven CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages on I-10 between El Paso and the Arizona line: Love’s #259 (Exit 132), Pilot #266 and TA Las Cruces #0014 (both Exit 139) in Las Cruces, Petro Deming #0385 (Exit 68), and Pilot #163, Flying J #690 (both Exit 24) and Love’s #276 (Exit 20) in Lordsburg. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves to or from Fort Bliss, White Sands and Holloman, RVs and snowbird fifth-wheels on I-10, and horse and stock trailers crossing the bootheel. West into Arizona, see Tucson / Benson / I-10; north on I-25, see Albuquerque / I-40 · I-25.

CAT Scales on I-10 in southern New Mexico

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Love’s #259, Pilot #266, Pilot #163, Flying J #690 and Love’s #276 say their stores are open 24 hours; Love’s #276 notes limited services and shorter weekend truck-care hours. TA Las Cruces and Petro Deming list CAT Scale among their amenities and say fuel is 24/7, but publish no 24-hour store statement, so we leave their scale hours blank. None of the seven publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. The ONE9 Dealer #1489 in Deming lists no amenities on Pilot’s page, so it is not here. Deming to Las Cruces (Exit 68 to Exit 132) is about 64 miles and Deming to Lordsburg (Exit 68 to Exit 24) about 44 miles with no other stop we have verified. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. New Mexico weighmaster rules differ from California — confirm ticket needs with your TO or licensing office. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

New Mexico ports of entry on I-10 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
