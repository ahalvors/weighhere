Public scales east of Albuquerque (Moriarty & Santa Rosa, I-40)

Three CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages on I-40 east of Albuquerque: Pilot #475 Moriarty (Exit 196), TA Moriarty #0229 (Exit 194) and TA Santa Rosa #0023 (Exit 277). Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves heading toward Amarillo, RVs and fifth-wheels on old Route 66, and horse and stock trailers crossing the eastern plains. West, see Albuquerque / I-40 · I-25 and Gallup / Jamestown / Milan / I-40.

This page lists 3 places to weigh near Moriarty & Santa Rosa, NM: 3 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Moriarty and Santa Rosa. 3 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales on I-40 east of Albuquerque

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Pilot #475 includes CAT Scale in its store offerings and says Open 24 Hours. TA Moriarty and TA Santa Rosa list CAT Scale among their amenities, but the hours they print are restaurant hours, so we leave their scale hours blank. None of the three publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. The ONE9 Dealer #1422 in Moriarty (Exit 197) does not list a CAT Scale, so it is not here. Santa Rosa to Tucumcari (I-40 Exit 277 to Exit 333) is about 55 miles with no stop we have verified yet. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh the tow vehicle for the trailer number. Do not unload horses at a truck stop. New Mexico weighmaster rules differ from California — confirm ticket needs with your TO or licensing office. Use CAT’s locator for other stops.

Do not go here for a ticket

New Mexico ports of entry on I-40 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
