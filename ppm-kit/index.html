The PPM Weigh Ticket Kit

Your PPM pays on the weight you can prove. This 23-page printable kit walks you through getting empty and full weight tickets that your finance office will accept, what to put on them, how the rules differ by branch, and what goes in the claim packet. It includes a weigh log and an expense tracker you can print and fill in.

What it is

It is a plain-language workbook built from published sources: DFAS, Military OneSource, the Personal Property Activity, Army, Navy and Marine Corps transportation office handouts, and CAT Scale’s own pages. Every rule cites its source, with the date it was checked (24 October 2026). Where the sources disagree (for example, whether a towed car is weighed attached or detached, or where Navy tickets must come from), the kit says so and tells you exactly what to ask your transportation office.

What it isn’t: official DoD guidance, a substitute for your counselor, or a calculator that predicts your payment. Your Personal Property Office owns the rules. The kit helps you ask the right questions and avoid avoidable mistakes.

Most of the weight-ticket basics are free in our PPM weight tickets guide and the one-page weigh-day checklist. The kit adds the branch-by-branch rules, the claim documents, and the printable checklists and logs you can have in hand at the scale.

The kit is a 23-page Letter-size PDF. Here is what it actually contains:

  6. If you can’t get a ticket: constructive weight and missing tickets
  7. Pre-move checklist

Frequently asked questions

Is this official?

No. WeighHere is an independent site and isn’t affiliated with DoD, any military service, DFAS or CAT Scale. The kit summarizes and links to official pages. Your transportation office has the final word.

Will it increase my PPM payment?

We can’t promise that. Your payment is based on the certified weight you move, up to your allowance. The kit is about not losing money to rejected or missing tickets, missed receipts, or missed deadlines.

Does it cover my branch?

It summarizes published guidance for the Army, Air Force and Space Force, Marine Corps, Navy and Coast Guard, and flags where it conflicts. Rules can differ by installation, so confirm with your office.

How current is it?

Sources were checked on 24 October 2026, and each one is listed with its link. Rules and CAT prices change, so check the linked pages before you move.

What format is it?

It is a 23-page PDF, Letter size. You can read it on a phone or print the checklists and logs.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the kit isn’t what you needed, email support@weighhere.com within 30 days for a no-questions-asked refund.

Is the free guide enough?

It might be. If you only need the weigh-day basics, read the free PPM weight tickets guide or print the free checklist. The kit is for people who want the whole process in one printable packet.
