Public scales around El Paso (Anthony, Santa Teresa & Horizon Blvd)

Five CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages around El Paso: Love’s #817 Santa Teresa on NM-136, Love’s #447 and Pilot #435 at I-10 Exit 0 in Anthony on the state line, and Flying J #728 and Petro El Paso at I-10 Exit 37 (Horizon Blvd) on the east side. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves to or from Fort Bliss, RVs and fifth-wheels on I-10, and horse trailers headed to or from Sunland Park.

This page lists 5 places to weigh near El Paso, Anthony & Santa Teresa: 5 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Santa Teresa, Anthony and El Paso. 5 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales around El Paso

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Both Love’s pages and both Pilot Flying J pages list a CAT Scale and say the store is open 24 hours; Petro El Paso lists a CAT Scale and 24/7 fuel but no 24-hour store statement. None publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Flying J #724 in Anthony (I-10 Exit 0) also lists a CAT Scale but Pilot shows it as temporarily closed, so it is not listed here. Anthony and Horizon Blvd are about 37 miles apart on I-10: west-side and Fort Bliss moves can use either, eastbound trips leaving town should weigh at Exit 37. Northwest on I-10, Las Cruces stops are on Las Cruces / Deming / Lordsburg / I-10. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle inspection sites, the New Mexico port of entry at Anthony, and the border commercial crossings at Santa Teresa and Ysleta are for commercial vehicles and international freight — not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
