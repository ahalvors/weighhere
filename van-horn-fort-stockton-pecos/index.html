Public scales in Far West Texas: Van Horn, Fort Stockton and Pecos

Six CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages across the long empty stretch east of El Paso: Pilot #209 at Van Horn (I-10 Exit 140A), Flying J #553 (Exit 259) and Love’s #542 (Exit 261) at Fort Stockton, and Pilot #1146 (I-20 Exit 39), Flying J #736 and Love’s #492 (both I-20 Exit 42) at Pecos. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves on I-10 or I-20, RVs and fifth-wheels headed to Big Bend or Carlsbad, and horse and stock trailers in ranch country.

This page lists 6 places to weigh near Van Horn, Fort Stockton & Pecos, TX: 6 CAT or truck-stop scales. CAT Scales in Van Horn, Fort Stockton and Pecos

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page. Every page here lists a CAT Scale and says the store is open 24 hours; none publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Distances are long out here: Van Horn is about 120 miles east of El Paso, Fort Stockton about 120 miles past that on I-10, and Pecos sits on I-20 about 55 miles north of Fort Stockton by US-285. Love’s #256 in Van Horn (Exit 140B) lists no CAT Scale, so Pilot #209 is the only verified scale there. West on I-10, see El Paso / Anthony / Santa Teresa. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Texas DPS commercial vehicle enforcement sites, roadside inspection pull-offs, and Border Patrol checkpoints on I-10 and US-90 are not a place to buy a civilian weighmaster ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
