Public scales near Gulfport, Biloxi and Moss Point

Six CAT Scales verified on the operators’ own location pages in south Mississippi: Love’s #619 at I-59 Exit 29 in Poplarville and Love’s #860 at I-59 Exit 1 in Picayune; then along I-10 on the Gulf Coast, Love’s #595 at Exit 28 and Flying J #676 at Exit 31 in Gulfport, Love’s #402 at Exit 44 north of Biloxi, and Pilot #586 at Exit 69 in Moss Point near the Alabama line. Useful for U-Haul and PPM moves around Keesler AFB, the Seabee base in Gulfport and Naval Station Pascagoula, RVs and boat trailers headed to the coast, and horse trailers coming down I-59 from Hattiesburg.

This page lists 6 places to weigh near Gulfport, Biloxi & Moss Point, MS: 6 CAT or truck-stop scales. They are in Poplarville, Picayune, Gulfport, Biloxi and Moss Point. 6 are listed as likely to print a certified weight ticket, the kind PPM movers and RV owners checking weight ratings usually need. CAT platforms have a 2,000 lb minimum and do not give corner weights.

CAT Scales from Poplarville to Moss Point

We list only stops verified on the operator’s own location page — I-59 north to south, then I-10 west to east. All four Love’s pages say the store is open 24 hours, and both Pilot / Flying J pages say Open 24 Hours. None publishes a CAT-staffing schedule. Love’s #595 Gulfport lists an RV dump, RV hookups and an RV fuel lane; Love’s #619 Poplarville lists an RV dump; Love’s #860 Picayune lists RV parking; Pilot flags Flying J #676 Gulfport and Pilot #586 Moss Point as RV-friendly. Love’s #402 Biloxi lists no RV amenities. 2,000 lb floor. No corner weights. Weigh the whole rig, then drop the trailer and weigh again if you need a trailer-only number.

Do not go here for a ticket

Mississippi commercial vehicle enforcement weigh stations and inspection pull-offs on I-10 and I-59 are for commercial vehicles — not a place to buy a civilian weight ticket for a U-Haul, RV, boat, horse trailer, or PPM load. Follow posted signs; do not treat this directory as a bypass guide.

Towing gear that helps before you weigh

As an Amazon Associate, WeighHere earns from qualifying purchases. Links open an Amazon search, not a specific product we tested.

  • Tongue weight scale — Check hitch weight in your driveway before the CAT run; a common target is roughly 10–15% of trailer weight on the ball.
  • Weight distribution hitch — Spreads heavy tongue weight across both axles of the tow vehicle so the front end stays planted on travel and horse trailers.
  • Trailer brake controller — Lets you set and trigger electric trailer brakes from the cab — worth having before a loaded trailer meets a long downgrade.
  • RV / trailer tire pressure monitor (TPMS) — Watches trailer tire pressure and heat while you drive; an underinflated tire on a heavy load is a common blowout.
  • Wheel chocks — Hold the trailer still while you unhitch, level, or measure tongue weight.
  • Moving hand truck / dolly — For PPM / DITY movers: load boxes and appliances between your empty and full weigh tickets without a second helper.
